# Area-1 installer coverage

The installer question is not yet closed, but five source-bounded abstract
attempt records are now separated from the genuinely live cases in
`proofs/InstallerCoverage.v`.  The formal results do not assume the missing
linked-Clight projection.

| Installer family | Checked result | What remains |

The principal conclusion is asymmetric.  Instances of the five abstract
record shapes are reduced to concrete linked-projection obligations, while
Ink's proposal survives its
local arithmetic and scheduling checks.  The authenticated timer-131 fixture
shows a conditional continuation, not how clean gameplay creates its initial
Graphics/Object separation.  Consequently this tranche neither proves a
retail counterexample nor eliminates Ink's idea.

The newer nonlocal State-first fixture changes the capability side of this
table, not the provenance side.  With collision Object at the upper warp and
State `(-1862,67314,-902)`, signed-short narrowing reaches the timer-131 top
in the arithmetic model.  The retail post-frame discriminator is consistent
with first-query success and no Graphics retry; the frame and later JP
lifecycle succeed.
Ordinary and action-phase PU movement run too late to install that
pre-collision split.  Pre-collision platform displacement is the identified
stock in-window 3D writer,
so the clean question still reduces to the open stock-projection/non-alias
bridge or one of its explicit escape classes.

The still-live physical/post-commit cases should not be folded into the Ink
gap obligation.  They can install the same final owner without first creating
that gap, and must be discharged by their own whole-program writer and object
lifecycle arguments.
