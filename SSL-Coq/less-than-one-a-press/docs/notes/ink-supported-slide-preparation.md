# Native landing and slide, with the box placed beforehand

14 November 2026. **The game can perform the landing, enter butt slide, and
produce the large low-collision/high-stored-graphics separation without
supplying a ground action. The Crazy Box can already be present before the
landing. This fragment still takes the ordinary warp, not the useful Ink
handoff.** Its first remaining failure is the following low floor lookup:
Mario remains over the static pad, so the high graphics-position retry does
not capture the spinning top.

This is a conditional predecessor, not a complete route. The initial high
airborne Mario and the box's earlier XYZ placement are supplied. Both the
late-box and before-landing-box versions were independently checked on the
original Japanese ROM. The earlier box version uses no non-controller
writes after its initial airborne cut. There is no held-A requirement.

## What this adds to the prior studies

The [supported late-push study](ink-mario-entry-runtime.md) found a supplied
high butt-slide state that keeps the action after a shove removes support,
then reaches a fresh low cache and the gap. This study replaces that supplied
ground action with a native airborne landing and the source's actual landing
cancellation. It does not repeat the heavily searched earlier phase-113
landing from [early air entry](ink-early-air-entry-crawl.md) or the older
[top-slide](ink-top-slide-to-crawl.md) and
[walking-edge](ink-walk-edge-crawl.md) studies.

The first successful version placed the box after Mario landed. A follow-up
requested by the user moved the actor cut before landing. Simply using the
same box height failed because it pushed Mario during the landing update.
Putting the naturally bouncing box lower lets it rise into contact one
update later. This removes the late actor write from the fragment, while
leaving the actor's actual delivery history open.

## Observed update order

M is Mario's movement position, C the object's collision position, and D
`gfx.pos`, the stored graphics position. D is not a claim about the render
matrix or where the visible model is drawn. Top timer in an end-of-update
snapshot has already advanced by one: the row for behavior 128 has stored
timer 129. These are the early-box version's exact representative values.

| Completed behavior | M = C | Stored graphics D | Action; floor/cache; platform |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 128: native landing | `(-1930,1839.883544921875,-875.0875244140625)` | Same | `FREEFALL_LAND`; top floor Y=1839.883544921875; platform 61 |
| 129: native slide entry and shove | `(-1946.9049072265625,1874.9208984375,-1000.1429443359375)` | Same X/Z, Y=1839.883544921875 | `BUTT_SLIDE`; retained top cache Y=1874.9208984375; platform NULL |
| 130: producer | Same X/Z, Y=768 | Same X/Z, Y=1874.9208984375 | `GROUND_BONK`; fresh static pad cache Y=768; platform NULL |
| 131: warp accepted | `(-1995,768,-1000.1429443359375)` | Same | `DISAPPEARED`, argument `0x40001`, used object 64; pad floor Y=768; platform NULL |
| Area 2 arrival | `(0,5500,256)` | Same | Ordinary entrance; no retained top |

After 128, the box is at Y=`1725.337646484375`, with vertical velocity
`8.454086303710938`; its collision slot is absent from Mario's list. After
129 it is at Y=`1729.791748046875`, vertical velocity
`4.454086303710938`; slot 49 is present and the native shove occurs. After
130 its Y is `1730.245849609375`; it no longer overlaps Mario at Y=768.
All three box floor caches remain the static roof at Y=1280. Those actor
positions and velocities are native updates, not intermediate edits.

Native platform carry matters. In the no-box observation, Mario's position
immediately before the behavior-129 action is
`(-1860.801025390625,1839.883544921875,-942.794189453125)`.
The actor placement was derived from this carried position, while contact
is still tested using the existing collision position. Copying the older
supplied slide fixture without accounting for this carry did not reproduce
the fragment.

The behavior-129 action log records `FREEFALL_LAND` followed by `BUTT_SLIDE`
and a ground movement step in the same update. The source's
`common_landing_cancels` tests `should_begin_sliding` before `INPUT_OFF_FLOOR`
(`mario_actions_moving.c:1758`); `ACT_BEGIN_SLIDING` is resolved to the
appropriate slide by `set_mario_action` (`mario.c:918`). The slide helper
performs the ground step and then alignment on its stopped/wall branch
(`mario_actions_moving.c:1359`). This is native slide execution, not merely
a surviving slide action ID.

At behavior 130 the selected floor is static Y=768 and the ceiling is the
top underside at Y=1523. The stopped step preserves high D before floor
alignment sets M to the low cached floor. The gap is
`1874.9208984375 - 768 = 1106.9208984375`.
At behavior 131, geometry resolution moves X to -1995 and the low query
still selects the pad. The source's high-position retry is conditional on
a NULL first floor (`mario.c:1314`), so this branch does not install the
top. The low-query explanation combines native end-update evidence and
source order; Wafel does not provide a floor-return breakpoint here.

## Controls, finite coverage and original-JP confirmation

The coarse preparation screen covers 2736 initial airborne cuts: phases
127/128, integer X=-2050..-1710 and Z=-1160..-800 in steps of 20,
one-unit old-plane clearance, yaw 0/16384/32768/49152, speed 20 and vY=-16.
2077 cuts reach an eligible native ground action with top-owned cache and
Y between 1700 and 2000 before 129. Its four actor target X/Z points do not
give a low-pad gap. The retained near miss lands in the Y=1280 roof region:
nominal target X=-1946 becomes -1945.95 after the native push, and signed
integer truncation matters at that terrain edge. The six-target refinement
finds two low-pad gaps and ordinary warps; moving the proposed X to -1947
is enough for the selected seed. This is not a global search or bound.

The early-box follow-up tests 97 explicit recipes. Six give the same
1106.9208984375 gap and ordinary warp, with box starting heights
`1716.883544921875 + i` for integer `i=0..5`, before behavior 128.
These are six tested points, not a certified continuous interval. The
same-height earlier placement at Y=1839.883544921875 fails: it contacts
Mario during 128, changing the landing to
`(-1939.719482421875,1841.7647705078125,-867.0310668945312)`; after 130
Mario still rides the top at Y=1844.4573974609375 with no large gap.
The tested actor-only cuts at phases 126/127, including position inversions,
do not give the gap. Their live activation/physics histories differ because
Mario remains at the distant prefix position until the phase-128 air cut;
they must not be read as a universal failure of earlier placement.

Every selected result is replayed from an independent fresh Wafel boot and
prefix. The coarse screen's retained case matches 33 update boundaries;
the six late-box targets match 186; the nine selected early-box cases match
264. The early receipt also preserves the four-update summaries for all
97 recipes. No full handoff/top capture is found in these finite menus.

The independent original-JP observer modes 4 and 5 both pass, each with
53 end-update observations: native landing, slide entry, low-cache gap,
ordinary warp and ordinary Area 2 arrival. Mode 4 places the actor before
129; mode 5 places it before 128 and makes no further state writes.

## Remaining boundary

Mario-side progress is real but local: a supplied airborne state now reaches
the actual ground slide and producer naturally, without a high ground-action
cut. Actor-side progress is that the box can exist before landing, moving
under its native bounce state. Joining both still fails to produce Ink:
the low query finds the pad and the top is not captured. Neither prerequisite
has a complete allowed delivery/approach history.

A HOLP throw was not performed in this study. A box delivered by a real
throw would have to reproduce suitable actual coordinates, velocity, held
state, collision activation and timing; reproducing XYZ alone is insufficient.
The next useful experiment is a supplied-held, native throw aimed at this
before-landing box state, with no actor writes after release, followed by
an approach search that retains the required low-floor miss at acceptance.
The existing high-HOLP delivery work is conditional and does not supply
that missing joined history. No new Coq theorem or global route claim is
asserted here, and the project's clean no-new-A route verdict remains open.
