# Hat-in-Hand and the unfinished three-actor placement join

15 November 2026. **Hat-in-Hand preserves remote HOLP through the tested
actual Box air throws. The full continuous placement history remains open.**
The ordinary hands-free dive/slide test was a different mechanism and does
not refute [Hat-in-Hand](https://ukikipedia.net/wiki/Hat-in-Hand). Further
search was stopped; this record preserves completed observations and the
steps that were planned, without presenting unrun work as a result.

## The distinction settled by the tests

The real436-input no-A eastern-box history reaches STOMACH_SLIDE while
holding Box47. Its next20 updates change HOLP every time. A remote-HOLP-only
grant is overwritten on the very next update. That is the ordinary
hands-free action/held-reference state, not Hat-in-Hand.

Hat-in-Hand instead retains MARIO_CAP_IN_HAND (0x20), with CAP_ON_HEAD (0x10)
cleared. Native cap-model processing derives handState3, whose right-hand
geometry has no held-object node. Consequently the held-object HOLP callback
does not run. The supplied cap state needs no per-update hand or rendering
patch. Four initial-cap controls preserve HOLP through actual AIR_THROW;
the paired ordinary-carry control overwrites it. Cap acquisition is supplied,
not reached by a controller history. The source claim requires the normal
valid-floor update to reach cap-model processing: a null-floor early return
can bypass it, so the flag alone is not an unrestricted freeze theorem.

## The planned sequence

1. **Use the lower working target.** Aim for the supplied-control geometry
   near MarioY1810 and shellY1800, instead of assuming a19000-unit ascent or
   a VPU journey is necessary. Reaching the actual entry remains a task.
2. **Park the initialized shell first.** Obtain its held reference, retain
   the required HOLP through Hat-in-Hand, and use Mario's actual throw.
   The frozen initialized shell can wait without a floor or later XYZ edits.
3. **Have Mario release the Crazy Box.** Use a native drop or a timed
   Hat-in-Hand air throw. Account for its initial velocity, drag, floor
   correction, distance activation and deletion, so it is alive for130.
   The throw target must compensate for subsequent flight, not merely place
   its release origin at the intended final shove point.
4. **Deliver the enlarged Bob last and approach holding it.** Real pickups,
   regrabs and travel must replace the supplied carrying/approach cuts.
   The verified final mechanism is descending shell contact on128: this
   natively drops the held Bob, enters ground riding, and fresh Z starts
   the slide. The same interaction pass does not also provide the Bob shove.
5. **Join the two shoves and the full handoff.** Bob129 must leave the
   position that lets the initial130 query cache768; Box130 then produces
   the useful stored-G/low-M split. Check warp acceptance, the high-G retry,
   top capture and retention, and the displaced Area2 spawn. An ordinary
   warp or a position gap does not qualify.
6. **Remove every intervening setup cut and validate on original JP.**
   Replay one continuous input history with A always released, retaining
   exact acquisition, HOLP and phase prerequisites. Then run a useful
   positive and an informative omission/timing control on the original ROM.

The immediate next finite experiment would have adjusted only the initial
Bob X/Z and Box throw HOLP to improve the129 height and130 endpoint in the
close Hat-in-Hand case. It was not run. A younger native Bob release profile
was considered but not tested. No controller route for Hat-in-Hand
acquisition, both pusher pickups/travel and shell approach was completed.
This was a staged investigation plan, not a known controller solution.
