# Real gameplay acquisition of the SSL pink Bob-omb

14 November 2026. **No real grab or clone of the native SSL Area 1 pink Bob-omb Buddy was found, and no Buddy was delivered to the useful Ink setup.** A continuous A-off gameplay history does reach a meaningful predecessor: talking to Buddy makes him Mario's remembered interaction target. Continuing that same history to a normal Crazy Box pickup replaces the remembered target with the box before Mario holds anything. It does not capture Buddy.

This investigation starts from proof commit `49807c387f90e29856e2ae34a656f9394137ec19`, branch `codex/ssl-pyramid-item-proof`; the remote branch was fetched and matched that revision. Decompile checkout `36fbf8d693a9fc2bdec0c77402f8e96d07d2f461` supplies the original-JP source, with the audited files unchanged from stock pin `9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af`. The prior [movement result](ink-buddy-movement.md) supplied a pickup-reference retarget. That edit is excluded here. The new experiments permit controller writes after the inherited debug level-selection start, with A released throughout; they do not supply position, action, actor placement, collision lists, `usedObj`, `interactObj`, or `heldObj`.

## What the new gameplay history reaches

Three references must be distinguished. `interactObj` identifies a current interaction, `usedObj` remembers a target for an action, and `heldObj` identifies the held item. Remembering Buddy after conversation is real gameplay progress to the first reference needed by the generic grab helper. It is not itself a pickup.

The complete replay has **3,471 A-off inputs**, including two neutral observation-tail inputs. It is field equivalence at those cuts, not whole-memory equivalence. Buddy stays live at his spawn and keeps his native behavior throughout, including the departure beyond his 3000-unit visibility distance. ## Why the obvious cloning variants do not solve this

The [source audit](ink-buddy-acquisition-source.md) checks all five callers of `mario_grab_used_object`. The helper itself is permissive: when Mario holds nothing, it copies `usedObj` to `heldObj`. The obstruction is reaching that call with Buddy still selected.

- **Dive, dive-slide and water pickup:** their successful selection checks require a current GRABBABLE object and replace `usedObj` immediately before grabbing. Buddy's native interaction type is TEXT, even though his script also has the HOLDABLE flag.
- **Ordinary delayed pickup:** it really can grab a vacant or replacement object slot. But entering it first remembers the selected GRABBABLE donor. Buddy's dialogue cannot replace that reference while the pickup remains pending. Leaving pickup does not preserve a hidden continuation that dialogue can resume.
- **Bowser pickup:** its entry also selects and remembers its target, and pending pickup rejects Buddy's dialogue callback. Bowser is not a stock SSL Area 1 actor.
- **Light grabbing or an edge action change:** a grabbable flag can survive action redispatch within one update, but it still accompanies the donor selected before the interaction pass stopped. No Buddy callback runs in the middle of that action loop. The next update clears the flags again.
- **Free a donor slot, then load Buddy into it:** the native Buddy is already alive in a separate occupied slot. Hiding him at a distance does not free it. The reviewed SSL code has no in-area Buddy constructor that would fill the donor slot; normal area reload also resets Mario's pickup state.

The last point is why a fake Goomba, Bob-omb or Crazy Box alone is insufficient. The player needs a concrete event that puts Buddy into the remembered slot, not merely a way to hold some vacant slot. The [external predecessor search](ink-buddy-acquisition-research.md) found relevant original explanations of fake pickup, interference and transport cloning, but no verified SSL Buddy capture history. Limited access to video descriptions is recorded explicitly; absence of a found movie is not an impossibility proof.

## Proof scope and remaining question

The earlier generated-Clight proof `ibm_pickup_actions_cannot_pass_npc_readiness` already rules out the three named pickup actions passing the actual NPC readiness helper. The new `iba_selector_without_grabbable_returns_false` constructs the path through the actual generated selector body when its current GRABBABLE input bit is clear: it returns false, emits no trace and leaves the entire memory unchanged. Its live Mario/input-load and execution premises are explicit; it assumes neither a skipped branch nor the value of the remembered target. `MainTheorem.current_ink_buddy_acquisition_boundary` connects these two local guards. Neither proves the entire game's reachable pointer invariant. Exact statements and pipeline checks are documented in [the acquisition proof record](ink-buddy-acquisition-proof.md).

The targeted build and selected discipline audit pass with Coq 8.16.1 / CompCert 3.15: 721 sources, no hole/link/integration problems, and seven existing allowed foundations for the new boundary. No project axiom or admission was added. The [archived audit](../../instrumentation/ink-buddy-acquisition-proof/summary.txt) establishes mechanical validation, not the unproved gameplay invariants.

The source argument closes a specific family: start at a complete update boundary with an unheld, live native Buddy that retains his TEXT/NPC identity and occupied slot, with actual collision provenance, stock action dispatch and ordinary initialization. An intermediate starting cut additionally needs its current grab-flag/target provenance. Within that family, the reviewed callers neither select Buddy directly nor substitute him during pending pickup. A full machine-checked all-history theorem would still need to connect those slot/type, collision, constructor and reset invariants through the execution model. This report does not assume those invariants as a proof of global gameplay impossibility.

The **closest verified predecessor** is completed native B conversation with `usedObj = Buddy` and `heldObj = NULL`. The **first failed condition** is reaching the generic grab while that same reference remains Buddy: ordinary pickup replaces it with the donor. Repeating broad B/stick scans after conversation has little value unless a concrete exception to that selection or slot-lifetime argument is identified. The next discriminating experiment would start from an actual in-area Buddy reload or a pointer-preserving transition outside the audited callers, log its constructor and slot history, and replay it with no reference edits. No such event has been found. Direct terrain movement of Buddy is a separate delivery question; it is not an acquisition result.

For the usable Ink setup, the verdicts remain separate: **Buddy acquisition: not demonstrated; Buddy delivery: not demonstrated; Mario/actor/top-phase join and displaced Area 2 arrival from one allowed history: not demonstrated.** The earlier useful three-actor replay still grants Buddy and Box placement, an initial Mario/shell setup, and a later Box relocation. This investigation does not remove those grants or convert an ordinary warp into spawning displacement. The full no-new-A route remains open, and all 45 subjective estimates remain unchanged, including Rank 1 **1–2%** and Ink Rank 2 **<1%**.
