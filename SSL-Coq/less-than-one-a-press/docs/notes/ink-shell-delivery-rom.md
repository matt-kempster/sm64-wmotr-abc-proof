# Original-JP pending-pickup shell timing pair

14 November 2026. **The original Japanese ROM confirms that the correctly timed native shell replacement can be carried, thrown, and mounted, while spawning it one update later leaves an intangible uninitialized shell that cannot be mounted.** Neither run assigns `usedObj`, `heldObj`, `riddenObj`, Mario's action/position, or an object's position/type/hitbox. Both still grant **one real shell-box action change** to make it spawn at the chosen time. This is a conditional low shell-cloning predecessor, not high delivery or an Ink handoff.

The mechanics and source audit are in [ink-shell-delivery-source.md](ink-shell-delivery-source.md). The evidence package is [jp-ink-shell-delivery](../../instrumentation/jp-ink-shell-delivery/README.md); its two archived raw logs, complete controller schedules, expected Wafel fields, check results, and hashes are reproducible without distributing the ROM or a save state.

## Verification and its explicit limit

The two expected traces were generated by freshly replaying the exported controller schedules and the one grant in the pinned Wafel0.8.5 DLL, then checked against the independently fresh-verified 49-case search and its followups. Native input schedules reproduced exactly. **All sampled Mario M/C/G, action, input, floor, platform, and top fields agree throughout the compared records**, subject only to the already-established Wafel-minus-native global-timer offset of1.

The first strict expanded check did **not** show complete equality of every added actor field: during untouched prefix polls372–833, the native small box has interaction subtype2 while Wafel has0, for462 observations in each run. Every other sampled field matches, and this subtype difference disappears at poll834, before the first actual small-box pickup finishes and almost1,000 updates before shell allocation. The raw logs and `initial-strict-check.json` preserve that failure. `INT_SUBTYPE_DELAY_INVINCIBILITY` is bit2; source overlap routines have a missing-return path, so a ROM/portable-runtime difference there is a plausible explanation, **not a proved diagnosis**.

The final checker reports `fullComparedFieldsMatch:false`, that exact prefix discrepancy, and a separate exact suffix result: **1,061 timely / 1,139 late observations from poll834 onward, zero mismatches across all compared fields**. It also requires the expected mount/no-mount outcome and the sole recorded grant. Its scoped pass must not be relabeled as whole-state, whole-history, or universal ROM/Wafel equivalence. No game state was edited to reconcile the difference.

The stronger conclusion therefore comes directly from the original-ROM trace: the useful initialized clone and informative failed initialization both occurred with native pending pickup and release. An above-contact high mount, useful Ink split, top capture, retained platform, displaced Area2 spawn, legal roof-box activation, high shell delivery, and a joined pusher history are all outside these two low delivery replays.
