# Eyerok/Mario contact probe manifest

## Authentication

- ROM: North-American Super Mario 64 (`baserom.us.z64`)
- MD5: `20b854b239203baf6c961b850a4a51a2`
- SHA-256: `17ce077343c6133f8c9f2d6d6d9a4ab62c8cd2aa57c40aea1f490b4c8bb21d91`
- Mupen64Plus CRC: `635A2BFF 8B022326`
- Emulator: Ubuntu-24.04 WSL `/usr/games/mupen64plus`, debugger interpreter mode (`--debug --emumode 0`)

The wrapper refuses a different ROM or WSL distribution. The concise traces
were generated by `analyze_contact_probe.py`; shared objects, raw logs, and
screenshots remain under the ignored `build/instrumentation/` directory.

## Evidence boundary

This is a source-shaped local microtrace, not a controller-only trace from
reset. The ROM executes all hand actions, hand movement, gravity integration,
ground collision, dynamic-surface loading, Mario geometry queries, action
handlers, landing/snap logic, and floor/platform selection. The probe never
writes either hand's position, velocity, gravity, movement flags, floor,
collision pointer, action, timer, previous action, or active flags.

The local setup deliberately writes:

1. `sWarpDest` to shorten Area 1 to Area 2 travel, then Mario position/velocity
   above the genuine Area 2 instant-warp triangle to enter Area 3.
2. The boss action/previous-action/timer to `WAKE_UP`. Both hands then execute
   their real `SLEEP -> IDLE` initialization and choose their real closed mesh.
3. After both real hand floor pointers equal arena Y `-1534`, boss scheduling
   fields `action`, `previousAction`, `timer`, `NumHands`, `ActiveHand`,
   `Unk104`, `Unk108`, `Unk110`, and `Unk1AC`. Mario is placed away from the
   hands while this scheduler fixture is installed.
4. Mario position/velocity and a ground action for one or more preload frames
   at the center of the closed top (`handY + 306`). The setup repeats until the
   ROM itself reports `mFloorObject == hand`, `floorHeight == handY + 306`, and
   `gMarioPlatform == hand`.

Mode-specific writes are:

- `stationary`: preload Mario uses `ACT_IDLE`; after authenticated contact no
  Mario state or controller field is changed.
- `b_only`: at hand action timer 2, Mario is reset to an interior point 125
  world units behind the authenticated center on the yaw-zero top, with the
  source-valid predecessor `ACT_WALKING`, forward speed `29`, face yaw `0`,
  and one `B + stickY=127` input. A remains released. The probe does
  **not** inject `ACT_DIVE` or positive Mario velocity; retail code must take
  `check_ground_dive_or_punch`, write velocity `20`, and enter `ACT_DIVE`.
- `held_a`: A is continuously down from Area 3 entry. Preload uses
  `ACT_MOVE_PUNCHING` with state 1 so the already-held level does not enter the
  kick early. At timer 2 the probe injects the source-valid local
  `ACT_MOVE_PUNCHING`, state-0 predecessor with zero forward speed. This is
  **not** a controller-authentic punch entry. Retail code must observe
  `INPUT_A_DOWN` without `INPUT_A_PRESSED`, enter `ACT_JUMP_KICK`, and write
  velocity `20`; B remains released.
- `held_a_b_edge`: A is continuously down from Area 3 entry and preload uses
  `ACT_IDLE`. At timer 2 the probe first requires Mario to remain idle at the
  authenticated hand-top floor/platform boundary, then supplies one new B
  edge. It makes no Mario-state write on that release poll. Retail code must
  execute `ACT_IDLE -> ACT_PUNCHING -> ACT_JUMP_KICK` in its same-frame action
  loop, with `A_DOWN` and `B_PRESSED` but no `A_PRESSED`, and must supply the
  jump-kick velocity itself.

## Derived trace columns

The concise CSV records Mario X/Y/Z, hand X/Y/Z, hand face yaw, collision
pointer, selected floor object's owner, platform, ceiling owner, input bits,
and both velocities. The analyzer inverse-transforms Mario X/Z into the
closed-hand local coordinates and tests the actual two top triangles
`(1,3,4)` and `(1,4,5)`. It also records:

- `verticalGap = handY + 306 - MarioY`;
- `within78`, a conservative pre-query buffer test using the previous
  completed Mario Y (not the TerrainData-cast intended quarter-step Y);
- `insideClosedTopXZ`;
- `sameHandFloor` and `sameHandPlatform`; and
- `contactClass`, distinguishing an ordinary same-hand snap from arena-floor,
  hand-ceiling, or other-floor selection.

These checks exclude seams and tunneling in the recorded microtraces.
The decisive contact evidence is the ROM-reported floor owner and platform;
`within78` explains the available vertical buffer but is not itself treated as
an exact reconstruction of the in-handler query height.

## Results

- A stationary Mario is inside the transformed top but the first `+85` step
  exceeds the `78` buffer. The arena floor wins, the hand underside becomes a
  dynamic ceiling, and Mario enters `ACT_SQUISHED`.
- The B-only predecessor makes retail code enter `ACT_DIVE`; Mario is at
  `-1208` with velocity `16`, then `-1192` with velocity `12`. It catches the
  `+85` hand at top Y `-1143` and remains on all positive steps
  `85,70,55,40,25,10`, reaching top Y `-943`. Every recorded point remains a
  strict interior point of the ordinary transformed top triangles.
- The held-A predecessor makes retail code enter `ACT_JUMP_KICK` with input
  `A_DOWN` and no `A_PRESSED` edge. It catches the `+85` hand and remains on
  all positive steps `85,70,55,40,25,10`, reaching top Y `-943`.
- The held-A+B mode removes the injected action predecessor: from
  authenticated `ACT_IDLE` contact and with no Mario-state write at release,
  one B edge makes retail code enter `ACT_PUNCHING` and then
  `ACT_JUMP_KICK` in the same frame. The observed input is `0x20a0`
  (`A_DOWN + B_PRESSED`, no `A_PRESSED`), and Mario again catches and rides
  all six positive steps to Y `-943`.

The traces prove local contact and action timing. The fourth mode also proves
the local action predecessor from authenticated idle contact using controller
input alone. They do not prove that normal controller play can reach the
staged boss schedule and initial hand contact, nor that Mario can dismount
onto the Area 3 warp at a useful height.
